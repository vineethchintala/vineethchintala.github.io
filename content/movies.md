## Perfect Endings in Cinema

> Once in a while a movie elevates itself with a perfect finish, with a final unforgettable impression. It can be the difference between going home satisfied and still remembering it years later. The difference between passing joy and lifelong love.

**Rules:** In no particular order. With few words and No spoilers!

{{< cinema-list >}}

#### 01. Chungking Express (1994)
Shows how effective, and playful, symmetry can be in storytelling. After all, nothing makes a relationship look more convincing than me becoming like you and you becoming like me.

#### 02. Portrait of a Lady on Fire (2019)
There is a parallel between art and love that is seamlessly intertwined in the movie. After all, love is also a journey of discovery. When the movie ends, you feel a sense of completeness not just in terms of story but as a work of art. Rewatching the movie will only confirm the feeling that the writing achieved everthing it set out to do. The magic has been transferred.

#### 03. Aftersun (2023)
Shared from the perfect distance. Or rather it becomes the perfect distance - you see, the story doesn't unravel; Instead it pulls you in close enough to sense the small clues of sadness, so that you start looking at its moments differently ...indirect yet precise! A remarkable debut. I will be watching everything made by Charlotte Wells.

#### 04. Nine Queens (2000)
From the many heist and con films I love, here is an Argentinian classic. I might have just as well picked a Soderbergh film but talking about Nine queens also helped me find housing in Barcelona :)

#### 05. The Truman Show (1998)
It’s probably one of the darkest concepts in the history of film. Perfect execution of the ending, capped with unsurpassed lightness.

#### 06. The Butterfly Effect (2004)
Perfect = good scene + right music. I have seen this movie only once, but the final minute found me a hundred times.

#### 07. The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (1966)
The classic! Longest movie on this list, but the ending brings complete satisfaction. Compelling, operatic, memorable, mischievous.

#### 08. Before Sunset (2004)
Small is beautiful, mood is everything. This is the soul of the Before trilogy.

#### 09. The Lives of Others (2006)
When words might not be sufficient, but movies are... It is always satisfying to see an honest payback, whether it's revenge or gratitude.

#### 10. Joint Security Area (2000)
How in a single final shot, we feel the full weight of the movie!

#### 11. Cinema Paradiso (1988)
Takes us back to the beginning, letting us feel the distance the story has travelled.
